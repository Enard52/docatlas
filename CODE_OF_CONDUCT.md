# Code of Conduct

DocAtlas welcomes contributors of different backgrounds, experience levels and abilities.
Treat questions and mistakes as opportunities to improve the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific and constructive in reviews and discussions.
- Critique the work without insulting the person.
- Make room for beginners and accept feedback in good faith.
- Respect privacy: use sample documents and redact credentials and personal data.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing and repeated
personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

For public incidents, flag the relevant comment to repository owner
[@nymav](https://github.com/nymav) without reposting private information.
For private harassment or platform abuse, use GitHub's reporting controls rather than
posting sensitive evidence in a public issue. This project does not offer a private
conduct-report inbox; do not assume that an issue is confidential.

The maintainer may ask for a correction, remove harmful content, issue a warning,
restrict participation temporarily or permanently, depending on severity and repetition.
Reports should be handled fairly, and reporters' personal information must not be shared.
This policy covers project participation and representing the project elsewhere.
